## The controls' 0.25

By elimination, the grader the controls pass is `skill-fired`. The replayed seed turn says
"Use the seven-steps-primer skill for this", so the Skill tool fires under every condition,
including the controls, whose SKILL.md sits under the same name. The two file graders
cannot pass without markers and the judge cannot pass a reply with none. `skill-fired`
measures the transcript, not the behaviour, and it puts a floor of 0.25 under every
condition on this case.

Under `--ablation none` the harness scores `tool_used: Skill` instead of demoting it, so
marking it with-only does nothing here. The choices are a zero weight, if the harness
accepts one, or removing the grader. Either way the case becomes a 3-grader score:
controls 0.00, treatment about 0.67 to 1.00.
