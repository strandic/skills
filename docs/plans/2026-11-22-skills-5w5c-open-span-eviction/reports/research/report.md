Report: can a tool.call hook's `next(e)` stay pending forever? (engine 2.1.330)

Sources. D = /private/tmp/claude-502/bundled-skills/2.1.330/ca1db19d763469e451f99ef08a23a4c3/plugin-authoring/types/claude-code.d.ts. R = reference.md in the same folder. All line numbers are for version 2.1.330. Nothing was edited and no scratch files were written.

Short answer. The docs say an interrupt, a deny or a tool error makes `next(e)` resolve; it does not stay pending. A hook beneath that hangs is also bounded, by the 20-minute budget. Nothing says `next(e)` stays pending forever. Nothing says it always settles in every case either. One real gap is in the mod itself: the `.finally` callback awaits `$.clock.now()` before it sets `span.to`. If that call rejects or never returns, the span stays open even though `next(e)` settled.
